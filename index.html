EVERY CITIZEN,
EVERY VOTE

Modernise Malta's voting
system for citizens abroad

Voting is a fundemental right, not a luxury reserved for those who can afford a plane ticket.


Right now, thousands of Maltese citizens living abroad are being locked out of our democratic process because of an outdated system.

The Cost of a Voice

With less than a month’s notice, overseas Maltese face an impossible choice: spend hundreds—sometimes thousands—of euros to fly home on short notice, or lose their voice entirely.

A Broken Band-Aid

Subsidised flights completely ignore anyone living outside specific routes. A citizen's right to vote should depend on their passport, not their bank account.

As Maltese citizens living abroad, we cannot vote where we currently reside. By keeping this outdated system, Malta is leaving its own diaspora politically homeless—unable to vote where they live, and blocked from voting where they belong.
